Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

This policy describes what OmniMind stores when you use omnimindmap.app. Contact support@omnimindmap.app for privacy questions or deletion requests.

Maps on your device

Local maps, drawings, and settings stay in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB). We do not receive those files unless you save them to the cloud or share them.

Account and cloud maps

If you sign in, Firebase Authentication handles the sign-in and Firestore stores your cloud maps under your user id. Share links let other signed-in people open a map you chose to share. Guest sessions use anonymous Firebase auth.

OmniMind Pro payments

PayPal processes the $9 one-time payment. We do not store your card number. After a verified payment our server stores the PayPal capture id, the buyer email from PayPal, and the Pro code. When you activate a code we also store up to 3 device ids for that code. Those records are not readable by the browser; only our server can read and write them.

Email

We send the Pro code with Resend from support@omnimindmap.app to the PayPal buyer email. That message contains the code and the buyer email.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not run a subscription billing profile. Payment details beyond the capture id, amount, and buyer email stay with PayPal.
